## How to play ?
 There are four types of cards in the game: Ace, King, Queen, and Jack. Both the player and the computer are dealt four cards each. At the beginning of each round, the system randomly selects a card type. You can play as many cards from your hand as you want. Your opponent can either believe you or call "BLUFF!"

If you get caught bluffing, you lose points. However, if you correctly catch your opponent bluffing, you earn points.

Be careful! Don't believe every lie, because you only have three lives!
